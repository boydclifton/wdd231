Master of the Medium

Explore the cinematic vision and interactive storytelling of Hideo Kojima.

The Architect of Tactical Espionage

From the PSX to the PS5, Hideo Kojima has continuously redefined what video games can be. By blending Hollywood-style cinematography with complex, fourth-wall-breaking gameplay, he created genres that did not previously exist.

This site catalogs his major directorial works and the design philosophy that makes a "Hideo Kojima Game".
