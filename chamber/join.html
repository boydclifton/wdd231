Non-Profit Membership

Free for registered non-profit organizations

Non-Profit Benefits

Non-profit members receive:

Bronze Membership

Ideal for fledgeling businesses working to gain a foothold in the business community

Bronze Benefits

Bronze members receive all benefits from NP tier, but also:

  • Discount of 15% on all chamber events
  • Directory listing that includes logo
  • Quarterly retreats with affiliated chamber organizations

Silver Membership

Perfect for mid-sized businesses seeking strong growth.

Silver Benefits

Silver tier members receive everything in NP and Bronze, but also:

  • Discount of 20% on all chamber events
  • Professionally designed premium directory listing with options for SEO links
  • Monthly "Business Spotlight" puff piece written by Jeremy

Gold Membership

Perfect for mid-sized businesses seeking strong growth.

Gold Benefits

Ideal for large enterprising businesses looking for explosive growth and brand recognition

  • Discount of 40% on all chamber events
  • Access to state-accredited lobbyists and institutional influence
  • Weekly "Business Spotlight" puff piece written by Jeremy
